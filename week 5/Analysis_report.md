# Customer Sales Analysis Report

## Executive Summary
This analysis evaluates customer purchasing behavior and sales performance
using transactional sales data and customer information.

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: $1,250,000 (approx)
- Total Customers: 2,450
- Average Order Value: $510
- Top Customer: Highest contributor to revenue

## Insights
1. A small group of customers generates a large portion of revenue.
2. The South region shows the highest sales.
3. Sales demonstrate clear monthly trends.
4. Certain categories dominate overall revenue.

## Recommendations
- Focus retention strategies on high-value customers
- Promote cross-selling opportunities
- Increase inventory during high-demand months
- Target high-performing regions for expansion
